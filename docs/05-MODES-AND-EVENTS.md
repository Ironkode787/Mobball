# 05 — Modes & Events

> **DISPATCH, 2:14 AM** — *"All units, the arcade again. Bring earplugs. And a warrant."*

Everything that interrupts, structures, or spices a Night. Each entry lists: trigger, what the
player does, and payout. Chance decides *what* fires; skill resolves it (P2).

---

## 1. Jobs ("Tonight's Work")

The mission system and the main ☆ Respect faucet. Up to 3 paper slips pinned to the board;
rerolls via the Consigliere. Drawn from a pool tagged by rank & owned features. Examples:

- *Shake the Block* — collect all 3 storefronts within one guy's run (☆10)
- *No Loose Ends* — finish a Night with zero briefcases left on the field (☆5)
- *Send a Message* — 6 bumper hits in 5s (☆5)
- *Quiet Money* — launder $50k in one Night without passing Heat 40 (☆15)
- *The Milk Run* — 3 Getaway Loops in one guy (☆10)
- *Bank Error* — win 3 casino bets in a row (☆20; gated so it's never the only slip)
- *Old Debts* — pinch-free Night (nobody drains before minute 3) (☆25)

**Story Jobs** (one per rank) are handcrafted multi-step sequences ending in a table-wide mode
(e.g. R3's "Made Man": light all storefronts, then the ceremony — 60s where every switch pays
☆ fractions). Story Jobs gate the Commission fight for that rank.

## 2. RAIDS (Heat 100)

Sirens. The music drops to drums. Doors slam. **60 seconds of survival pinball:**

- Cop targets pour in: 4 "beat cop" standups near the flippers (they *push* the ball toward
  outlanes if left standing — knock them down), a paddy wagon parks on an outlane, and the
  **Captain's magnet** periodically yanks the ball drain-ward (audio telegraph 1.5s prior;
  counterable with a well-timed flip or Lean).
- Survive (keep at least one guy alive) → **Beat the Rap**: keep everything, +☆25, Heat resets
  to 30, headline, and a big clean-cash "exhibit A returned" bonus.
- Fail (all deployed guys pinched during the raid) → confiscation: lose 30% of held dirty
  (upgrade-reducible), guys serve real stretches (bail ×3), Heat resets to 0. Never touches
  clean cash or upgrades (P5: setbacks sting, never erase).

**Anticipation is the design point:** from Heat 90 the whole presentation leans in — bass drops
out, red wash creeps up the table edges, the scanner (if owned) mutters. Players describe the
feeling as "greed vs. sirens." That's the game working.

## 3. Collection Rounds

Trigger: all 3 storefront banks armed simultaneously. 25s timer: collect all three in any
order; finishing lights **Double Collection** on the last one. Perfect round = ☆10 + the
pizzeria throws a pizza across the table (full-screen physics gag, +$).

## 4. The Wire (numbers draws)

Every ~90s of play, the tote board draws 00–99. Your ticket = last two digits of the spinner's
session spin count — **the spinner is the bet slip**, so grinding the spinner lane both earns
and re-rolls your number. Last-digit match pays ×6 dirty; exact pays ×80 **clean**. Wiretap
upgrade (see [04](04-UPGRADE-TREE.md) E-T5) shows the next draw 15s early — suddenly the
spinner lane is a precision instrument under a clock. Skill from chance.

## 5. HEISTS (R5+, from the Docks war room)

Planned, bespoke, high-stakes table modes — the game's setpieces. Choose a target, choose an
approach (**Quiet** = tighter shots & Heat-free / **Loud** = generous shots, big Heat), pick a
crew guy as the "inside man" (his trait modifies the mode), pay a stake, run it.

Each heist is a scripted shot-sequence with a fail-forward structure (missing a beat degrades
the take, only a drain ends it):

| Heist | Sequence sketch | Take |
|-------|-----------------|------|
| **The Payroll Truck** | stop the truck (3 hits) → crack door (drop bank) → getaway loop ×2 | clean, small |
| **The Museum Job** | disable 3 spotlight sensors (timed standups between sweeps) → glass case (captive ball ×4) → rooftop ramp exit | relic (Museum meta) + clean |
| **The Casino Vault** | count cards (hit 5 lit slots in drawn order) → vault wheel (spinner ×20 in 10s) → walk out the front (SLOW ball through lit lane — a *gentleness* skill check!) | huge clean |
| **The Train** | Docks crane onto moving container platforms (moving targets) → uncouple (double-flip timing) → tunnel escape | dirty mountain + a unique guy |
| **The Evidence Locker** (Federal era) | steal your own case file: raid hardware active but PASSIVE unless you touch a spotlight — a stealth pinball mode | deletes 50 Federal Heat + ☆ |

Heists are limited by "casing" (one heist per target per N Nights) so they're events, not farms.

## 6. THE COMMISSION (rank-up bosses)

Each rank R3+ ends with a rival family encounter — pinball boss fights on your own table,
using your geometry against you. Beat them to take the rank AND absorb a **signature upgrade
that cannot be bought** (collection pressure for prestige runs). All fights are multi-phase,
no-timer (pressure comes from the table, not a clock), and pause the economy (pure skill).

| Rank | Boss | Gimmick | Signature spoil |
|------|------|---------|-----------------|
| R3 | **Sammy Two-Flippers** | his crew *jams one of your flippers* in 6s pulses (telegraphed by a wrench gag) — play one-flipper pinball on demand | *Sammy's Spare* — once/Night, instant flipper un-jam + 1 free Lean |
| R4 | **The Butcher** | armor-plates your bumpers (worthless) until you break the meat truck circling the field | *Cold Storage* — bumpers store value while disabled, pay double when freed |
| R5 | **Madame Fortuna** | casino witch: reverses a random rule each phase (slings pay Heat, laundromat DIRTIES money) — read the table, adapt | *Marked Cards* — see roulette result 2s early |
| R6 | **The Silent Don** | the audio mutes except HIS cane taps — play a phase on visuals + haptics only (accessibility-checked: visual telegraphs are complete) | *The Quiet Word* — Sit-Down freezes Heat 90s instead of 60 |
| R7 | **The Old Kingpin** | plays YOUR table against you: he owns your upgrades too — every feature you bought is armed against you (your kickbacks eject at you, your tunnel eats your ball once). The final exam is your own build | *The Ring* — +1 Juice per future boss beaten; his chair in the penthouse |

## 7. THE RAT (late-game story event)

Once The Rat specialist has been hired (or R6 reached, whichever first), a slow-burn arc: odd
static on the scanner, one pigeon wearing a wire, a briefcase that photographs the table. Then
a Night flagged **"Something's Off"**: three suspects sit in the backglass; clues surface via
play (the laundromat total is short; Manny grabs an award and *pockets* part; a payphone rings
— answer it with a ball hit). Accuse via target choice:

- Right → flip him: keep the +100% income, betrayal risk gone, ☆50.
- Wrong → the real rat triggers an instant half-strength Raid; try again in 3 Nights.

Pure theater built from existing systems; the design cost is dialogue and a clue table.

## 8. ELECTIONS (Puppet Mayor, T6)

A multi-Night campaign mode: "canvass" each district by completing its zone objective within a
Night (alley, corner, block, club, docks — 5 districts, any order, progress persists). When all
five are lit: **Election Night**, a 90s frenzy where every switch is a "vote." Win →
**City Hall Administration** for the next 5 Nights: cops become escorts (Own-the-Precinct
behavior even without the upgrade), raid threshold moves to 120, storefronts auto-arm.
Lose → a recount headline and one district keeps its light. Re-runnable each "term."

## 9. FEDERAL HEAT & THE RICO RAID (R7)

The blue meter (100–200). Accrues slowly from empire size itself (you're too big to ignore —
a gentle, thematic soft-timer toward prestige, never a hard wall). At 200: **the RICO raid** —
a 2-minute, 3-phase super-raid (street sweep → wiretap phase where sound cues drop out one bus
at a time → the Director's double-magnet finale). Survive: the single largest payout in the
game + "UNTOUCHABLE" headline + Federal meter −100. Fail: standard confiscation ×2 and the
strong suggestion that it's time to **Skip Town** ([06](06-PRESTIGE-AND-CITIES.md)) — prestige
is always the player's choice, but the city is visibly closing in.

## 10. Small events & rituals

- **Mystery Briefcases** — odds & upgrades in [03](03-ECONOMY.md) §3. Delivered by a guy in a
  trench coat who walks it onto the field. If ignored 60s, he *leaves with it* (fear of missing
  out capped at one shrug).
- **The Phone** — the backbox phone rings occasionally: answer (tap) for a time-limited offer:
  a bet, a job, a tip ("the wagon's coming"), or your nonna (hang up = −☆1. Answer = buff.)
- **Sunday Dinner** — daily bonus: the family table sets itself with one random dish-buff for
  the day (+wash rate / +☆ from jobs / +briefcase odds…). Streaks widen the *choice* (pick 1 of
  2, then 1 of 3) rather than raising numbers — daily ritual without FOMO pressure (P5).
- **Winter / festival dressing** — cosmetic table seasons on a calendar; no mechanics.
