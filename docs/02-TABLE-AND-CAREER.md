# 02 — The Table & The Career

> **CITY PLANNING OFFICE, MEMO** — *"Re: the arcade on 5th. It has a second floor now. It did
> not have a second floor on Tuesday. Please advise."*

The table **is** the career. This doc walks the eight ranks and the physical playfield each one
bolts on. One continuous vertical playfield (portrait), roughly one phone-screen tall at Rank 0
and about three screens tall plus two mini-fields by Rank 7, with the camera following the ball.

**The vertical metaphor is strict and legible:** the gutter is at the bottom, the penthouse at
the top. Climbing the ladder means literally sending the ball higher. The drain is the street
that swallows nobodies.

---

## 0. Reading the table

```
                        [ CITY HALL DOME ]          ← R7
                     ┌───────────────────────┐
                     │      PENTHOUSE        │      ← R6
                     │   (the Commission)    │
       ┌─────────────┴───────────────────────┴──┐
       │            THE CLUB (upper deck)       │   ← R4  casino mini-field,
       │         ─ mini flippers, roulette ─    │        own mini-flipper
   ┌───┴────────────────────────────────────────┴───┐
   │  DOCKS        THE BLOCK (midfield)             │ ← R3/R5
   │ (mini-field)  storefront banks, orbits, ramps  │
   │   cranes ─┐                                    │
   └───────────┴────────────────────────────────────┘
       │           THE CORNER  (numbers, wire)      │ ← R1/R2
       │        THE ALLEY  (bumpers, slings)        │ ← R0
       │      ┌── L flipper      R flipper ──┐      │
       │  outlane│                     │outlane     │
       └────────┴───────  DRAIN  ──────┴────────────┘
                        (the street)                 launch lane: THE DROP-OFF →
```

Every zone follows one contract: **a zone = a racket cluster = a music stem = a light rig = a
set of shots with one job each.** Zones dock onto anchor sockets on the core table scene
([09-TECH](09-TECH.md) §4), so "the empire grows" is literally modular scene assembly — new
geometry slides in with a construction animation (scaffolding, tarps, a crew of little guys
with hammers) during The Count. The player watches their money become architecture.

## 1. Rank ladder overview

Ranks are gated by **Respect ☆ only** (skill-earned — see [03](03-ECONOMY.md) §5). Cash builds
the empire; Respect proves you deserve it. Each rank-up triggers: a Commission encounter (boss,
from R3 up, [05](05-MODES-AND-EVENTS.md) §6), a table expansion, a mugshot/portrait evolution,
a newspaper front page, and a new music stem.

| Rank | Title | ☆ Respect | Table expansion | New loop unlocked |
|-----:|-------|----------:|-----------------|-------------------|
| R0 | **Lookout** | 0 | The Alley: bare table | Flip, earn, survive |
| R1 | **Errand Boy** | 10 | Numbers lane + Job board | Jobs (missions), Lean |
| R2 | **Numbers Runner** | 50 | The Corner: Wire target, payphones | The Wire draws; laundering v0 |
| R3 | **Soldier** | 150 | The Block: 3 storefronts, left orbit | Protection collections; first boss |
| R4 | **Capo** | 400 | **THE CLUB** — upper deck | Casino gambling = laundering v2; 2-ball Family Meeting |
| R5 | **Underboss** | 1,000 | **THE DOCKS** — lower-left mini-field + right orbit | Smuggling; Heists; kickbacks |
| R6 | **Boss** | 2,500 | **PENTHOUSE** — top level | The Commission table; Elections; 3-ball |
| R7 | **Kingpin** | 6,000 | **CITY HALL** dome + full illumination | Empire Mode; Federal Heat; 5-ball Reunion; Skip Town |

Pacing target: R0→R1 in the first 15 minutes, R3 by end of day 1, R4 day 2–3 (the "whoa, the
table has an upstairs" moment must land inside the first three sessions), R7 around day 12–18
of normal play. Prestige loops compress all of this (see [06](06-PRESTIGE-AND-CITIES.md)).

## 2. Zone by zone

### R0 — THE ALLEY (the bare table)

The machine as found: two flippers, **one** dented trash-can bumper, battered dead sling
triangles, short inlane return sweeps, bare wood, and a single hand-painted sign. The
slings have no kick, score, Corner Boy figures or glow yet; the long vertical inlane guards
are still missing. It should feel like a garage-built machine — because each upgrade's
physical job is the tutorial. The first upgrades you buy are the furniture:

- *Second and third trash can* (bumper cluster — they're literally trash cans; hits knock
  garbage value out of them)
- *Corner Boys* (power the dead slings — two lookouts in folding chairs who kick the ball back)
- *Guard Rails* (extend the short returns with vertical inlane guards), *Chalk Lines* (lane
  lights), *A Real Plunger*.

Buying pinball's standard parts one at a time teaches what each part does — the genre's
furniture becomes the incremental game's first tier. First laundering: none yet. Dirty cash
only, tiny clean allowance (see [03](03-ECONOMY.md) §2: "Pocket Money").

**Shots:** bumper nest (earn), starter sling triangles (survive; Corner Boys later power them),
Drop-Off lanes ×2.

### R1 — THE JOB BOARD + NUMBERS LANE

A left-side lane with a **spinner** (a bicycle wheel — the numbers game runs on it). The Job
board (three paper slips on the wall art) activates missions. At the start of each Night, the
pre-Night **Roll Call** screen shows those active slips under **Tonight's Work**, then shows the
available Bench guys. The player selects the crew and the selection order explicitly sets who
serves first, second, and third; a short available roster starts with all of its guys (there is
no dead-end waiting for three). Lean/nudge unlocks with a tiny Inspector intro. **Shots:**
spinner lane (steady dirty trickle per spin — first "aim for value" decision).

### R2 — THE CORNER

Table widens ~15%. Adds **The Wire** — a standup target bank of three payphones and a lit
**numbers board** (00–99). Every ~90s a number draws ([05](05-MODES-AND-EVENTS.md) §4): the
spinner's accumulated spin count sets *your* number — skill sets your ticket. First laundering
feature arrives as an upgrade: **Lucky's Laundromat** storefront (see R3 geometry, unlockable
early) with a slow wash-cycle loop. **Shots:** payphones (place bets), spinner (set number),
laundromat loop v0.

### R3 — THE BLOCK

The midfield. Three **storefront drop-target banks** across the table's waist:

- **Lucky's Laundromat** (launder loop: passing the ball through the open door converts a
  chunk of dirty→clean; the door is open when its 3 targets are down)
- **Nonna's Pizzeria** (collection: knock the bank down to "collect" — instantly cash out
  minutes of that racket's idle income; smells like garlic bread and money)
- **Fat Tony's Pawn** (mystery: cycles buffs — sell junk for dirty, buy Inspector patience,
  fence briefcase items)

Plus the **Getaway Loop** (left orbit — full-speed orbit shots build combo speed and "escape"
value; late upgrades make it charge the Tunnel, see [04](04-UPGRADE-TREE.md) T5). First
Commission fight before the rank-up: **Sammy Two-Flippers** ([05](05-MODES-AND-EVENTS.md) §6).

**The storefront banks are the protection racket**: each collected bank re-arms after a cool-
down; a timed "Collection Round" mode lights all three ([05](05-MODES-AND-EVENTS.md) §3).

### R4 — THE CLUB (the table grows UP)

The landmark expansion — scaffolding rises over a Night's Count, and next Night there's an
**upper deck**: a half-width casino mini-playfield perched top-right with its own small
flipper, reached by **The Staircase** (right ramp, a Clean Hit makes it reliably).

On the deck: a physical **roulette pocket wheel** (spinning hole-set the ball drops into),
**slot drop-targets** (three reels of 3 drop targets; knock matching rows), and the **High
Roller saucer** (bet-multiplier hold). The casino's economics are the laundering endgame:
**you gamble dirty cash and winnings come out clean** — the house washes it for you, because of
course it does. Odds start house-favored; Influence upgrades tilt them your way
([03](03-ECONOMY.md) §3, [04](04-UPGRADE-TREE.md) branch E).

Choosing to *keep the ball upstairs* (high variance, laundering, mini-flipper skill) versus
*downstairs* (steady rackets) becomes the core mid-game decision — the promised loop change.
Also new here: **Family Meeting** (2-ball multiball) via the back-room saucer behind the slots.

### R5 — THE DOCKS

Lower-left mini-field behind a one-way gate: crane magnet, three **container stacks** (drop
banks), a **cargo ramp** back to the main field, and its own outlane risk (falling off the
pier). Smuggling runs: light containers in sequence while "the shipment is hot" — timed, loud,
Heat-heavy, huge dirty payouts. The right orbit ("Truck Route") and **kickback outlanes**
("boys on the corner") arrive with this rank. Heists unlock ([05](05-MODES-AND-EVENTS.md) §5).

### R6 — THE PENTHOUSE

Top level, full-width, glassy and quiet compared to the noise below (audio contrast — the most
dangerous room is the calmest). Five **chair targets** around a long table: the Five Families.
Claiming chairs (multi-Night persistent progress) runs the Commission endgame and unlocks
**Elections** ([05](05-MODES-AND-EVENTS.md) §8). 3-ball Family Meeting. The Penthouse saucer
starts **"Sit-Down"** negotiations: freeze Heat for 60s, double storefront collections.

### R7 — CITY HALL (the dome)

A golden loop crowning the table — the hardest, longest shot in the game (full-table left-orbit
→ staircase → penthouse gate → dome loop, chained). Completing the **City Hall Circuit** lights
**Empire Mode**: every feature lit, every stem playing, ×10 on everything, 5-ball Family
Reunion available, 60 seconds, re-lightable. This is the sensory-overload crown jewel and the
skill-check it hides behind keeps it earned (P4 + P2). Federal Heat and Skip Town live here
([03](03-ECONOMY.md) §4, [06](06-PRESTIGE-AND-CITIES.md)).

## 3. How upgrades change geometry (beyond new zones)

Three physical upgrade classes, so the table visibly evolves *between* rank expansions:

1. **Additions** — new hardware in existing zones: a second spinner, a captive ball ("the
   hostage negotiation"), extra kickback, the Tunnel entrance, saint statues (Black Book).
2. **Transformations** — same shot, better fiction & function: trash cans → dumpsters →
   armored collection trucks (bumper value tiers); bicycle wheel → tote board → stock ticker;
   the laundromat gaining neon, then a second floor, then a fleet of vans (wash rate tiers).
3. **Rule plates** — visible brass plaques bolted near a feature when its rule upgrades
   ("Two Books", "Concrete Shoes") — the table wears its build like a rap sheet, and an
   opponent could read your build from a screenshot. (Also: free legibility, P4.)

## 4. Table personality details (cheap, high-charm)

- Little guys with hammers assemble every purchased upgrade during The Count.
- The Inspector sighs visibly when you buy his bribe upgrades. (He keeps the money.)
- Storefronts change window dressing with the in-game season; the pizzeria puts up a
  "GRAND RE-RE-RE-OPENING" banner each prestige.
- Pigeons on the penthouse rail scatter when the ball passes. One of them is wearing a tiny
  wire. (Foreshadowing The Rat. Players will find it frame-by-frame and love it.)
- The drain isn't a hole; it's a storm grate lit from below. At Federal Heat, red-and-blue
  light leaks up through it.
