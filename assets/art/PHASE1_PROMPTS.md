# Phase 1 ImageGen prompt set

Provider: built-in ImageGen. Generated 2026-08-30. All people are fictional. Every output
is intentionally opaque because the provider returned checkerboard pixels instead of real
alpha on transparency attempts; circular props use a deterministic runtime shader mask.
No generated text is used—the game draws every word, number, and state mark itself.

All prompts shared this art direction: late-1970s East Coast working-class noir; premium
hand-inked paperback-crime illustration; subtle halftone and paper grain; tobacco, cream,
charcoal, oxblood, restrained teal, and aged brass; no logos, trademarks, readable text,
real-person likenesses, or checkerboard patterns.

## P1-01 — Eastport backglass

Wide opaque smoked-black glass plate, inset aged-brass frame, seven incandescent chase bulbs,
restrained deco sunburst edges, and a generous empty center for deterministic title copy.
Straight-on, no perspective, no words or symbols, near-black outer edge.

## P1-02 — Count room

Portrait 9:16 overhead count-room still life: green banker lamp at upper left, black adding
machine at upper right, stacks of worn ledgers at the sides, a large blank central paper tally
surface, and an intentionally dark lower action zone. No writing or loose currency.

## P1-03 — Trash-can bumper

Strict top-down circular battered galvanized trash-can lid with concentric steel ribs, small
tarnished-brass cap, believable dents and grime, isolated on uniform ink-black, no handles.
Bold enough to read as a roughly 60-pixel live table object.

## P1-04 — Bicycle spinner

Strict top-down stripped bicycle wheel, dark steel rim, sparse spokes, worn rubber edge and a
tarnished-brass hub, isolated on uniform ink-black. Mechanical, grounded, and legible small.

## P1-05 — Payphone bank

Straight-on vertical 1970s public payphone face in blackened steel and aged brass, receiver,
coin slot and rotary/number hardware as shapes only, tight rectangular crop on black, no copy.

## P1-06 — Job board

Straight-on rectangular corkboard in dark walnut with aged-brass corners, blank cream index
cards and paper slips pinned by red and black pushpins, generous blank areas for live UI copy.

## P1-07 — Laundromat

Straight-on compact neighborhood facade: dark brick and teal enamel, three glowing round
washer windows, blank cream signboard, one dark doorway, near-black outside edge.

## P1-08 — Pizzeria

Straight-on compact facade: dark brick, worn oxblood enamel, warm oven glow, round pan
silhouettes, blank cream signboard, one dark doorway, serious rather than whimsical.

## P1-09 — Pawn shop

Straight-on compact facade: soot-dark brick, brass window cage, blank cream signboard,
silhouettes of a radio, trumpet, watch, and toolbox, plus a barred dark doorway. No weapons.

## P1-10 — Starter 01

Fictional working-class man in his late 30s, weathered jacket, guarded expression; chest-up
4:5 neighborhood-file portrait with cream border, tobacco-gray ink and paperback halftone.

## P1-11 — Starter 02

Fictional working-class woman around 40, sharp dark bob, pea coat and skeptical intelligent
expression; same opaque 4:5 neighborhood-file card treatment as Starter 01.

## P1-12 — Starter 03

Fictional Black man in his mid-40s, close-cropped hair, neat mustache, worn leather work
jacket and calm direct gaze; dignified, non-stereotyped, same 4:5 card treatment.

## P1-13 — Starter 04

Fictional Irish-American woman in her early 50s, curly auburn-brown hair lightly streaked with
gray, wool coat, faint eyebrow scar and perceptive unsmiling expression; same 4:5 treatment.
