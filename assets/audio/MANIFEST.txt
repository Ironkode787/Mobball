KINGPIN — generated audio manifest
==============================================================================

Everything below is synthesised by tools/audiogen (numpy/scipy only). No samples,
no external assets, no licences: we own every byte. Regenerate with
    python3 tools/audiogen/generate.py
The generator is deterministic — a re-run reproduces these files exactly.

Levels: -1.5 dBFS is a ceiling, not a target. SFX peaks form a deliberate ladder
(knocker and kickback loudest, spinner_tick and wall_tap far below) so gameplay code
does not have to ride volume_db on every call; music stems are matched by integrated
loudness (LUFS) instead, so the stack stays balanced as stems fade in. The orderings
the ladder actually promises — the rank-up pair over the Club's jackpot, the jackpot
over the Family Meeting stingers — are asserted against the written files at build
time and again in tests/test_audio_assets.gd, not left as a comment.

SFX — assets/audio/sfx/*.wav  (44.1 kHz, 16-bit PCM, mono)
Looping events are built periodic and carry a 'seam' figure; every other file is
a one-shot that starts and ends at true zero. Loops: bill_counter, siren, wheel_clatter.
Wave 4 closes the vocabulary with the endgame (specs/m3-fall-rise.md AUDIO-4):
the Commission fights, the Docks, the Penthouse, the dome, the heists, the
election, Empire Mode, the Reunion, the briefcases and Skip Town. Two of its peak
placements are load-bearing and both are asserted below the table: dome_loop is
the biggest PITCHED sound in the game and still loses to the knocker, and
empire_start — everything lit, x10 on everything — still loses to the rank-up
pair, because a mode starting never outranks a career moving. Four files have a
LENGTH contract as well as a level one: wrench_telegraph and crane_telegraph are
exactly as long as the tells they cover, and skip_town/train_away are timed
against AudioDirector.play_farewell(), so all four are played at pitch 1.0.
