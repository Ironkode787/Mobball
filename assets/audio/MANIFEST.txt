KINGPIN — generated audio manifest
==============================================================================

Everything below is synthesised by tools/audiogen (numpy/scipy only). No samples,
no external assets, no licences: we own every byte. Regenerate with
    python3 tools/audiogen/generate.py
The generator is deterministic — a re-run reproduces these files exactly.

Levels: -1.5 dBFS is a ceiling, not a target. SFX peaks form a deliberate ladder
(knocker and kickback loudest, spinner_tick and wall_tap far below) so gameplay code
does not have to ride volume_db on every call; music stems are matched by integrated
loudness (LUFS) instead, so the stack stays balanced as stems fade in. The orderings
the ladder actually promises — the rank-up pair over the Club's jackpot, the jackpot
over the Family Meeting stingers — are asserted against the written files at build
time and again in tests/test_audio_assets.gd, not left as a comment.
