## Identity

- Category: Game / Arcade
- Business model: premium; no ads; no in-app progression purchases

## Short description

`Flip, launder, and build a criminal empire directly onto your pinball table.`

## Full description

Every point is money. Every upgrade changes the machine.

KINGPIN combines portrait pinball with a deep criminal-empire progression game. Start with a
bare alley table, skim dirty cash from skill shots, wash it through physical targets, and spend
the clean take on rackets that bolt new districts onto the playfield.

Your pinballs are named crew members. Drains get them pinched. Jobs, raids, bosses, casino
machines, smuggling runs, and the Ledger force a constant choice: earn more, wash safely, or
ride the Heat while the table gets louder.

- Responsive 120 Hz pinball physics built for touchscreens
- A playfield that physically grows with your career
- Dirty and clean money connected by shots—not menus
- Named crew, bail, traits, specialists, and boss Nights
- Layered original music that expands with your empire
- Reduced-motion, reduced-flash, subtitle, haptic, and mix controls
- Premium game: no ads and no pay-to-win progression

The city remembers every racket. The drain remembers everybody.
